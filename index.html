How I work

A point of view
01 · Habit by design

Loops, not funnels

I design with Nir Eyal’s Hook Model in hand — trigger, action, variable reward, investment. Every flow here is annotated with the behavioral reasoning behind each screen, because retention is a design decision long before it’s a metric.

02 · Ornament meets gallery

A real aesthetic point of view

My visual language pulls from Indian folk painting — Madhubani suns, Pichwai lotuses — and the calm of Seattle’s contemporary museums. Rich work on quiet walls. Strong illustration earns attention; restraint keeps it.

03 · Words are the interface

Content design, not copy-paste

A button label can win or lose a signup. I treat microcopy as a primary surface — plain language, benefit-first framing, error prevention over error messages. The clearest interface is usually a sentence, well chosen.
